Things to know

Running cost

Estimates for peak summer months. ISEER 5.3 is the 2026 five-star threshold for splits; 4.1 is three-star. Compare labels by ISEER, not stars: a 2025 five-star is a 2026 four-star.

Before the electrician comes

Installation checklist
  • Dedicated circuit; never shared with the geyser or kitchen points.
  • Outdoor unit: 30 cm clear behind, 60 cm on the service side, open space in front. Never inside a closed grille box.
  • Keep the copper pipe run short; most 1.5-ton units allow about 15–20 m, 5–10 m without extra gas.
  • Most 2026 inverter ACs work from about 160–280 V without a stabiliser. Add one only if your supply swings outside that.
  • Refrigerant R32 or R290 only. Never top up with a different gas.
  • In hot-dry cities, pick a model that promises full capacity at 43–46 °C.
How this is calculated

Simplified ASHRAE/ISHRAE room heat balance: wall and roof conduction with solar-equivalent temperature differences, glass solar gain with shading factors, people, lights, appliances, kitchen spill, and air leakage (sensible and latent), plus a 10 % safety factor. Run for the city's peak dry-bulb design day, a monsoon humid day, and optionally a +3 °C heat wave; the largest wins. Wall U-values follow BEE Eco-Niwas Samhita figures; city design temperatures are ASHRAE Handbook 2021 values (annual 0.4 % dry-bulb with coincident wet-bulb for the peak case; annual 0.4 % wet-bulb with coincident dry-bulb for the humid case). Inverter sizes are accepted between 90 % and 125 % of the load; fixed-speed between 100 % and 115 %. Cities marked * borrow the nearest station or are estimated. This is a household guide, not an engineering certificate.

Engine v. Coefficients for solar gain, kitchen spill and monsoon design points are tuned engineering estimates and should be treated as ±10 %.
